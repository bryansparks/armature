# Self-Evolving Agent Harness — Formalizing Harness RSI

**Date:** 2026-10-27
**Source:** arXiv:2610.18813v1 (submitted 2026-10-13), Chuyang Wei, Haoxiang Guan, Minghang Zhu, Kexin Zhou, Jiyan He, Xiren Zhou, Huanhuan Chen, Yifei Shen.
Primary sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2610.18813v1 (HTML build truncated after §3.3 — Sections 4–6 read via the project repo, github.com/zgcagi/Harness-RSI: README, `docs/evidence-criteria.md`, `docs/reported-evidence.md`, 66-page full report in `paper/`). CC BY 4.0.
**Question:** A 66-page framework + theory + evidence-audit paper on the exact loop `armature improve`/`armature tune` implements: revising an agent's software scaffolding (prompts, memory, workflows, executable code) while model weights stay fixed, with accepted revisions persisting across runs. What does it formalize that the shelf hasn't, and does any of it change the parked improve-revision design?

---

## 1. What the paper is

Not a new mechanism — a **formalization and an audit**. Three parts:

**(a) Framework and taxonomy (§2–3).** A harness H is the software/config governing a fixed model M's execution. The lifecycle is a batch update loop: `H_k = Update(H_{k−1}, B_k)` where `B_k` is a batch of b rollouts (§2, Eqs. 2–3). Two requirements define genuine self-evolution: operational feedback with update continuity, and **persistence across runs** ("accepted revisions are stored and loaded in a subsequent run"). The Update operator "combines diagnosis, proposal, assessment, and admission" (§2) — unadmitted candidates leave the incumbent in place (`H_k = H_{k−1}`). Update targets split into *interpreted state* (instructions, stored experience, task-level routing) vs *executable revisions*: tools/code patches (§3.1), repository/runtime logic (§3.2), and "Everything Is a Plugin" — capabilities generated, validated, and mounted at runtime via declared interfaces (§3.3). Candidates may revise internal critics but **"cannot modify J," the final assessor** — the evaluator is structurally outside the candidate's editable surface. Scope is honest: "self-evolution" (the update process) is distinguished from "self-improvement" (a gain established by comparison), and the process is defined "without assuming that each revision helps."

**(b) Theory (§4, via repo — HTML truncated).** Candidate search is mapped to evolutionary optimization (variation, selection, archive retention, budget allocation) and examined "through continual PAC-learning." Three results: an expected generalization bound; a retention bound conditional on historical regression budgets (statement lives in the PDF, not the README); and a **high-probability admission rule**: for at most K candidates per round over T rounds, compare each candidate with the incumbent on **m fresh tasks independent of its construction**, and accept when

> `Δ̂_k(H') + √(2 ln(KT/δ)/m) < 0`

where Δ̂ is candidate loss minus incumbent loss. This gives joint confidence 1−δ for accepted candidates on their round's task mixture. Assumptions: independent exogenous task draws, task losses in [0,1], fixed model and evaluator. Two stated caveats matter: the gate covers only the tested candidate (including merged edits), and **"repeated rollouts reduce execution noise but do not add independent tasks."**

**(c) Evidence audit (repo, `docs/`).** 95 methods graded on one primary reported comparison each, across four criteria (`evidence-criteria.md`):
- **Selection** — the reported estimate is unselected; outcomes stay separate from proposal/ranking/admission/report choice. Full pass = prequential evaluation (each outcome scored *before* it can influence an update) or test-time adaptation without benchmark-verdict access. Evaluator/scorer protection from candidate edits is a structural requirement under this criterion.
- **Activation** — the accepted update actually *operated* in the scored runs, on three levels: **Available** (entered the run) → **Reached** (the changed path executed) → **Carried** (its effect reached the intended downstream use). Requires "reached or carried evidence tied to the primary comparison" — execution beacons or adherence measurements.
- **Attribution** — isolate the update, control inference resources (matched budgets over calls/tokens/turns/pass@k/reasoning effort), and show a margin beyond measured execution variation: a 5%-level paired test, an interval excluding zero, or > 2 standard errors over independent runs.
- **Improvement** — grades *task distance* between reporting tasks and update-forming tasks: held-out/unseen-benchmark full credit; only-tasks-that-formed-the-update fails.

**Audit headline (`reported-evidence.md`):** of 93 reports with empirical evaluation, **0 meet all four criteria** — 26 meet 0, 27 meet 1, 33 meet 2, 7 meet 3. Only **8 fully meet Activation** and **10 fully meet Attribution**. The most common profile is met Selection + task distance with *partial* Activation and Attribution. Representative grading: FlowEvo is a rare strong case (✅/✅/✅/◐, 3 seeds, paired McNemar, cost moving against the gain); GPTSwarm fails Selection (20 problems used to optimize AND evaluate) and its ~1.5-SE margin doesn't clear noise; Agent KB's 18.7-pt headline is mostly pass@3 vs pass@1.

**(d) Borrowed evaluations (§3–4, Tables 2–4).** The paper's own numbers are drawn from prior-work case studies (SkillOpt/SkillOpt-Lite, shen2026skilloptlite), not new experiments: HarnessOpt + skill lets **GPT-5.4-nano (77.58%) beat GPT-5.5 with SkillOpt (76.20%)** on SpreadsheetBench — harness optimization compensates for a model tier; HO+skill beats SkillOpt-Lite by 5.69–11.39 pp across all four models. The EIP case: first-task tool calls drop **13 → 3**, but wall time +54.9% and tokens +15.7% on that first task; cumulative savings over five tasks reach 110,351 tokens / 22.97 s. The authors themselves flag inconsistent first-task cost reports and decline to merge them into one trajectory.

## 2. Position against the shelf

**This is the theory backbone for the improve-revision brief** (assembled from 2610.30499 + Harness² + 2610.29458). It doesn't contradict anything; it *formalizes* the shelf's convergences:

| Shelf convergence | This paper |
|---|---|
| Parent-as-candidate + abstention (2610.30499, improve.py:882 gap) | **Formalizes it.** The incumbent is the null hypothesis of the admission rule; acceptance requires beating it on m fresh tasks by more than the uniform-deviation term √(2 ln(KT/δ)/m). Abstention falls out as the default branch (`H_k = H_{k−1}`). |
| Probe-and-contrast, paired candidates (Harness², 2610.29458) | **Supplies the statistical formality.** Attribution criterion: paired tests over task outcomes, matched inference budgets, margin > 2 SE. The brief's "behavioral contrast" now has a named threshold. |
| Dev/held-out separation (2610.30499's G₂ = 5.62) | **Names the mechanism: prequential evaluation.** Score each outcome before it can influence an update — a concrete structure for improve's drift trigger, stronger than "use a held-out set." |
| Evaluator independence (L3 critic, 2610.34577); mark/verifier separation (Octopus) | **Same rule, sharper scope: candidates cannot modify J.** Third statement of the governance convergence, now aimed at the improve loop specifically. |
| Field evidence is weak (Raw-RAG App. E sandbagged baselines; 2610.30499 improvement ≈ 0) | **Systematizes it: 0/93 reports meet all four evidence criteria.** The audit is the literature-scale version of both papers' complaints. |

**Genuinely new to the shelf — the Activation criterion (Available/Reached/Carried).** Nothing prior gave us this vocabulary. It names the campaign H2 failure mode exactly: corruption didn't degrade operational HQS because the system couldn't see whether anything it changed *fired*. It also names H4-v2's pain: passively injected memory is **Available without Reached evidence** — loaded into context but never shown to execute or carry; the nav arm (agent-pulled) is Reached/Carried *by design*. The criterion converts "agent-pulled beats passive injection" from a latency argument into an evidence-class argument. Audit fact: only 8/93 reports have full Activation evidence — the field ships changes it never demonstrates executed.

## 3. Applicability to Armature

| Paper mechanism | Armature status | Concrete slice |
|---|---|---|
| Admission rule `Δ̂ + √(2 ln(KT/δ)/m) < 0` on m fresh tasks | **Missing where it matters.** improve.py's `_pick_best_proposal` uses an ε-band fuzzy tiebreak (:322); nothing requires a fresh-task margin. | When the improve revision lands (brief item #1), use this as the promotion test: K candidates per round, T rounds tracked, m validation runs *never touched by diagnosis*, δ budgeted. Replaces heuristic abstention with a thresholded one. |
| "Repeated rollouts do not add independent tasks" | **A direct warning for the campaign/soak harnesses.** Our repeated-evaluation instinct (3 trials per arm) reduces noise but adds zero statistical power for admission. | Validation sets for improve must vary *task inputs*, not just seeds. An m-run gate over identical inputs is the audit's GPTSwarm failure. |
| Prequential evaluation (Selection criterion) | **Partial.** Improve's drift trigger scores runs it then feeds back into the next update — selected evidence. | Structure improve's loop prequentially: every completed run is scored and archived before any update decision sees it; promotion evidence must come from runs scored before the proposal existed. |
| Activation: Available/Reached/Carried beacons | **Missing.** No mechanism records whether an accepted spec change's touched path actually executed in the scored runs. | Cheap slice: when improve applies a change, log the diff surface (stages/tools/keys touched); the next run's trace marks whether those paths executed and whether downstream stages consumed the change. Carry the verdict into the next diagnosis bundle — this is also the H2 fix (self_improve can't fire on what it can't see). |
| Attribution: matched inference budgets | **Partial.** H4-v2 showed a latency term can cancel a quality gain — the paper says this is the audit's most common hole (82/93 partial). | Every improve candidate comparison should report the inference envelope (tokens, calls, tier) per arm; a candidate whose gain needs more budget is a cost-frontier point, not a win. |
| Candidates cannot modify J | **Partial.** Locked-surface violations are rejected in improve, but the *judge/eval criteria themselves* aren't explicitly on the locked surface. | Make the campaign/improve assessor's rubric (HQS weights, verdict schema) part of the immutable locked surface in the improve revision. |
| Update-target taxonomy (interpreted state / tools & code / runtime logic / plugins) | **Already the architecture, now with a map.** role descriptions + context_layers = interpreted state; adapters = tools & code; DAG topology = workflows & topologies; factory Tier 2 = repository & runtime logic; the workflow registry = declared plugin interfaces. | Use the taxonomy as the organizing axis for the six-dimension harness diagnosis and the factory tier ladder; EIP's validate-then-mount is the registry's phase-D shape. |
| HarnessOpt: nano + HO beats GPT-5.5 + SkillOpt (77.58 vs 76.20) | **Charter citation for the whole improve/factory bet** — harness quality substitutes for a model tier. | Caveat: borrowed from prior-work case studies, not independently rerun here; cite as supporting, not primary, evidence. |
| EIP: 13→3 tool calls, +54.9% wall first task, cumulative savings by task 5 | Design input for the workflow registry / factory capability generation. | The first-use cost bump then amortization is the expected profile for registry-derived capabilities — plan the eval to measure cumulative, not first-task. |
| Four-criteria audit as a self-check | **New idea, zero build cost.** Grade Armature's OWN claims (H1–H4, soak, pyramid eval) against Selection/Activation/Attribution/Improvement. | One exercise: score the H4-v2 report — likely met Selection and task distance, partial Activation (memory Available, not Reached) — the criteria would have named the latency finding before the run did. |

## 4. Honest limitations of the paper

- **A framework/survey/audit, not a new empirical result.** Its headline numbers are other people's experiments re-reported; the EIP case is a simulated office-spreadsheet workload.
- **The audit was LLM-performed** (Opus 5.5, with targeted consistency checks), not independently human-annotated — the paper's own note.
- **Theory assumptions are strong:** independent exogenous task draws, losses in [0,1], fixed model and evaluator. A real fleet (ours) has drifting task distributions, changing evaluators, and mixed-magnitude losses — the bounds are directional guidance, not free license. The paper itself concedes "reliable deployment requires continual validation as task distributions and evaluators change."
- The retention bound's exact statement is in the 66-page PDF (HTML build is broken past §3.3); this note carries only its shape ("conditional on historical regression budgets").
- The EIP/HarnessOpt authors overlap with the SkillOpt authors being evaluated (self-reported numbers).

**Verdict:** shelve as a build candidate, import as the **theory backbone for the parked improve revision**. The brief's two items now have formal statements — the admission inequality for item #1 (parent-as-candidate/abstention becomes a thresholded test on m fresh tasks) and the Attribution criterion for item #2 (probe-and-contrast becomes paired tests with matched budgets and a > 2 SE margin) — plus one genuinely new import (the Activation Available/Reached/Carried beacon, which also names the H2 and H4-v2 failure modes) and one free one (self-audit our own claims against the four criteria). The four-criteria audit independently systematizes what the shelf already believed: nearly the entire harness-RSI literature fails basic evidence hygiene, which is the strongest argument yet that Armature's controlled multi-arm campaign harness is the right measurement instrument.

**Note for the improve-revision brief:** add three entries — (1) admission inequality `√(2 ln(KT/δ)/m)` replacing the ε-band; (2) prequential structure for the drift trigger's evidence; (3) Activation beacons in the post-change diagnosis bundle, with the locked surface extended to the assessor rubric.
